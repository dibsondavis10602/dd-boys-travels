Meet Leo,
your desktop buddy.

A small white Pomeranian who lives on your PC screen and helps you out: reminders, timers, to-do lists, notes, maths, weather, quick answers, and a nudge to drink water. Feed him, play fetch, and watch his best friend Guru drop by.

Free · Windows 10 or 11 · about 100 MB

⏰ Never forget

“Remind me to call Mum at 6 pm.” Timers, focus sessions, water and stretch breaks.

✅ Stay on top

To-do lists, notes, and a “plan my day” briefing with your weather.

🧠 Gets smarter

Learns your routines and suggests the right thing at the right time.

How to install

  1. Click Download Leo for Windows above and wait for Leo-Setup.exe to save.
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click Leo-Setup.exe.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. Leo is new and not signed yet, so Windows doesn't know him.
  4. Leo pops up at the bottom of your screen. Click him to say hi!
Tips: press Ctrl+Shift+L to talk to Leo from anywhere and Ctrl+Shift+H to hide him. Right-click him to feed him, play fetch or call Guru. Your notes and reminders stay on your own PC.
